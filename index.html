01 · What we found

What we saw when we looked at GetYourGuide on social.

Before writing anything, we looked at how people talk about GetYourGuide and travel experiences on TikTok and Instagram. Four things stood out.

$1B+ revenue

The leading marketplace. The creator layer can go further.

GetYourGuide crossed $1B in revenue and booked 10M experiences in Q3 2025 alone. The brand already runs creator campaigns and has an 8,000-member creator community, but most organic content on TikTok is still user-made clips, not briefed creators with install hooks.

TikTok is the search engine

Travel planning starts on TikTok now.

TikTok is the #2 platform for travel planning after Google. One in three TikTok users have made travel plans from a video. Searches like "best things to do in [city]" and "hidden gems [destination]" get millions of views, and GetYourGuide can own that space with the right creators.

TikTok GO partner

Already inside TikTok's booking layer.

GetYourGuide is a booking partner on TikTok GO, meaning users can book experiences directly on TikTok. Creator content that links to a bookable experience closes the loop from discovery to install in one tap.

13.6M downloads in 2024

App installs grew 19% year over year.

The app hit 13.6M downloads in 2024, up 19% from the year before, with 91.8M total iOS installs and a 4.9-star rating. Creator-driven campaigns with install hooks can accelerate that growth in the US and Germany.

Sources: PhocusWire on revenue milestone; Business of Apps on download data; TikTok GO partnership; TikTok travel planning stats. Checked November 2026.

04 · A wildcard worth testing

Four creator types outside the usual travel crowd.

Next to the shortlist, we'd keep a small part of the first wave for four creator types that most travel brands never think about. A few creators each, measured like the rest. If one works, it becomes a channel of its own.

Wildcard 1

Food challenge creators

Not your typical foodies but creators who do "I tried every X in [city]" challenges. Food tours are one of GetYourGuide's strongest categories. A challenge format turns a booking into a video series that keeps performing.

"I tried 5 food tours in one day in Barcelona. Which one was worth it?"
#foodtour · #foodchallenge · #triedeverything
Wildcard 2

History and "did you know" creators

Creators who tell the stories behind famous landmarks. Facts and secrets that make viewers say "I need to see that". Guided history tours are a huge category for GetYourGuide, and this format drives saves and shares.

"The dark secret behind this famous landmark — and you can actually visit it."
#didyouknow · #historyfacts · #hiddenstory
Wildcard 3

Family travel creators

Parents documenting trips with kids and looking for activities the whole family can enjoy. GetYourGuide has a family-friendly filter and hundreds of kid-friendly experiences. This audience books more per trip and shares recommendations with other parents.

"3 experiences my kids actually loved in Rome (no boring museum tours)."
#familytravel · #travelwithkids · #familyfriendly
Wildcard 4

Art and street art creators

Creators who do walking tours of street art, graffiti and creative neighborhoods. GetYourGuide lists street art tours in Berlin, London, NYC and more. Visual, shareable content that reaches an audience travel brands usually miss.

"I found the coolest street art tour in Berlin and here's what happened."
#streetart · #arttour · #urbanexploring
How we'd test it: two or three creators per wildcard inside the first wave, each with their own link, so you can compare cost per install against the main shortlist. We'd scout these creators after our call.
